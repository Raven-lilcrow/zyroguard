Where is your shop?
Lakbay Bikes is at 45 Osmena Boulevard, Cebu City, across Robinsons Fuente. Our second shop is at IT Park, Lahug.

What are your opening hours?
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, repairs only, no bike sales.

Is there parking?
Free parking for 6 cars at the Osmena shop. The IT Park shop has paid parking in the building.

Do you have group rides?
Yes, every Saturday at 5:30 AM from the Osmena shop. 30 to 40 km at an easy pace, beginners welcome. Helmets are required.

Can I bring my bike inside the IT Park shop?
Yes, use the service entrance on the ground floor.
