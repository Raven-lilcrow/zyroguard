# Lakbay Bikes Help Center

## Repairs and tune-ups

### How much is a tune-up?
A **basic tune-up** is P650: brakes and gears adjusted, chain cleaned and oiled, tires checked.
The **full tune-up** is P1,450 and adds wheel truing, bearing service and a full wash.

### How long do repairs take?
Most repairs are done the same day if you drop the bike off before 12:00 noon.
Wheel builds and suspension service take 3 to 5 days.

### Do you fix e-bikes?
Yes, we service e-bike brakes, tires and drivetrains. We do not repair motors or batteries.
We send those to the brand's service center for you.

## Warranty

| Item | Warranty |
|---|---|
| New bike frame | 1 year |
| New bike parts | 6 months |
| Repair work | 30 days |

The warranty does not cover crash damage or normal wear like brake pads, chains and tires.

## Buying a bike

We sell road, mountain and city bikes starting at P12,500. You can test ride any bike in stock,
just bring a valid ID.

### Do you have installment plans?
Yes. Pay in 3, 6 or 12 months with BillEase or a credit card. 3 and 6 months are 0% interest.

## Payment

We accept cash, GCash, Maya, Visa and Mastercard.

## Online orders and shipping

Parts and accessories ordered on our website ship anywhere in the Philippines:

- Cebu: next day, P80
- Other provinces: 3 to 6 days, P150
- Free shipping on orders over P2,000

Bikes are pickup only, we don't ship whole bikes.

To track an order, open **My Orders** on lakbaybikes.ph and tap the order.
